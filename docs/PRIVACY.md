# RevealMe — Privacy & GDPR Readiness

Privacy must be designed around the fact that identity and visual media may be withheld initially but are still sensitive data.

## Principles

- Collect only what is needed.
- Keep private media private by default.
- Make consent explicit.
- Make reveal reversible where technically and socially appropriate.
- Provide deletion and access controls.
- Minimise retention.
- Document the purpose of each data category.

## GDPR readiness

A production launch in the EU would require a proper legal assessment, lawful-basis analysis, privacy notice, data-processing design, deletion/export mechanisms and appropriate controller/processor arrangements.

This document is a product-design starting point, not legal advice.
