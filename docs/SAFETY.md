# RevealMe — Safety & Moderation

A real social product must assume that some users will misuse interaction features.

## Required production capabilities

- Block
- Report
- Unmatch/disconnect
- Abuse detection
- Rate limits
- Moderation workflows
- Account controls
- Age-appropriate design
- Escalation for serious safety incidents

The reveal mechanic must never become a tool for coercion, harassment or unwanted exposure.

Safety is a product requirement, not a post-launch add-on.
