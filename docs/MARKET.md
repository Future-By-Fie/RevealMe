# RevealMe — Market & Differentiation

## Categories to examine

RevealMe should be compared honestly with:

- anonymous and pseudonymous social products
- dating and social-discovery applications
- profile-first social networks
- blind/limited-profile matching experiences
- privacy-first communication products

## Differentiation hypothesis

The opportunity is the **sequence of interaction** rather than anonymity alone:

**interaction first → identity reveal later**

The concept should be tested against existing products before making strong novelty or market claims.
