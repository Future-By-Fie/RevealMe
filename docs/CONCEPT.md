# RevealMe — Concept

## The insight

Most social discovery is appearance-first. Profile photo, name and public identity are exposed before there has been any meaningful interaction.

RevealMe flips that order.

**Meet before you reveal.**

## Core interaction

**Connect → interact → reveal → continue**

A person can enter an interaction without immediately exposing their complete visual identity. The system creates a small amount of curiosity and context first; the reveal then becomes an intentional moment.

## Why it matters

The concept explores whether reducing the pressure of first-impression identity can create a different kind of social discovery: more curiosity, more interaction and potentially more meaningful connection.

This is a product hypothesis, not a validated behavioral claim.

## What RevealMe is not

It is not intended as anonymity for abuse, a dating-only product, or a replacement for ordinary social profiles. The concept needs strong safety, reporting and consent mechanisms before real-world deployment.

## Differentiation hypothesis

The differentiator is not simply hiding an image. It is making **reveal an interaction state** rather than a static profile property.
