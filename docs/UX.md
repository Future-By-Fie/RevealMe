# RevealMe — UX & Reveal Mechanic

## Primary flow

**Discover → Connect → Interact → Reveal → Continue**

### Discover

The user encounters another person without immediately receiving the full visual identity.

### Connect

A lightweight connection starts the interaction.

### Interact

Conversation/personality becomes the first layer of discovery.

### Reveal

A deliberate reveal moment transitions the interaction from partial identity to fuller identity.

### Continue

After reveal, the relationship can continue as a normal social connection.

## Design principle

The reveal should feel earned, mutual and memorable — not like a settings toggle.

## Safety UX

Users need clear controls for decline, block, report, disconnect and consent. Reveal should never be forced by inactivity, pressure or hidden defaults.
